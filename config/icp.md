# Ideal Customer Profile

We sell virtual assistant (VA) training courses through Elevate University.
Our students are individuals who want to become virtual assistants.
We want to find organizations that reach these people and could promote or partner with us.

- Who they are: Career coaches, remote work communities, job training programs, women's and military spouse career groups, work-from-home blogs and newsletters, freelance and side-hustle educators, and VA job boards
- Size: Small to medium (anything from one coach to a team of 50)
- Countries: United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, Philippines
- Who we contact: Owner, founder, community manager, partnerships manager, or the person in charge of marketing
- Good signs: Teaches or writes about working from home, has an email list or active community, already promotes courses or affiliate programs, posts often, has a "partner with us" or "work with us" page
- Bad signs: Sells a competing VA course, inactive for over 6 months, no public business contact, sites that are mostly spam or ads
